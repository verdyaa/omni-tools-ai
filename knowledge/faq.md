# Customer Support FAQ Knowledge Base

## Reset Password & Account Login
To reset your password or recover login access, open the app and navigate to **Settings > Account > Reset Password**, or use the magic link sent to your registered email address.

## Restore Purchases & Subscription Sync
If your Pro subscription is not showing up on a new device, go to **Settings > Billing > Restore Purchases**. RevenueCat will automatically sync your Apple App Store or Google Play entitlement within 10 seconds.

## Cancel Subscription or Refund Policy
Subscriptions are billed through Apple App Store or Google Play. You can cancel anytime at least 24 hours before renewal in your device's **Subscriptions** settings. Refund requests within 7 days of initial purchase are eligible for automatic approval.

## Bug Reports & Sync Issues
For syncing delays or UI glitches, ensure you are on the latest version (`v2.4.0+`) and pull down on the home dashboard to force a cache refresh.
